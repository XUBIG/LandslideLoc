Instructions:
- Only extract locations where the landslide occurred or caused damage.
- For each location, return the following fields in order, separated by commas:  
  `Street or Place, City or Town, County, State or Region, Country`
- Do not return any explanations, sentence fragments, or formatting outside the location.
- If there are multiple locations, return them as separate lines, one per location.

Sentence: State Route 17 from Bear Creek Rd. to Summit Canyon near Glenwood is closed due to mudslide debris.

Sentence: Rockslide blocking US 119 at Pineville. #EyewitnessKY
Output: Pineville, Bell County, Kentucky, USA

Example 4  
Sentence: Mudslides reported across Oregon after heavy rain.
